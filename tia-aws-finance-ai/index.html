Analyst + action agent

Finix reads the general ledger, receivables and payables, explains what's moving and why, and acts on it with a person approving every step.

Budget & trends

Monthly and half-year position against budget, with the biggest variances called out and tracked over time.

Variance drivers

Drills any category down by department, vendor or event, with the reason recorded in the books.

Receivables & collections

Finds overdue invoices by age and amount, and starts collection follow-ups once you confirm.

Payables & approvals

Routes vendor invoices to the right approver under your approval limits.

Vendor risk & payment controls

Holds payments to vendors with unverified bank details and offers a verification task instead, so policy is applied every time, not just when someone remembers.

Fits your finance stack

Designed to plug into QuickBooks, NetSuite, SAP, Oracle and Microsoft Dynamics through AWS integrations, so answers come from the systems your team already uses.
